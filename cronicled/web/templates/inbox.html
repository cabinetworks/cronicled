{# cronicled/web/templates/inbox.html #} {# One proposal is one block, not a table row. It was a table, and the buttons sat in the last cell of a row that was then followed by up to four full-width detail rows. Reading down the page, a proposal's buttons appeared BELOW the previous proposal's detail lines and ABOVE its own filename, so they looked like they belonged to the row beneath. That was misread in use -- reported as "the top result showed a button the first time and not the second" -- and on a page whose buttons write to a media library, a control that appears to belong to the wrong row is a hazard rather than an untidiness.
